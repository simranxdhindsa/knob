# Code of Conduct

Knob is made by people who love good sound and good software. We want everyone who takes part, in issues, discussions and pull requests, to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, never on the person.
- Respect different backgrounds, languages, skill levels and opinions.

## What is not okay

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately derailing a conversation.

## What happens

Comments, issues or contributions that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed. Repeated or serious cases can lead to a ban from the project.

## Reporting

If someone makes you feel unwelcome, contact the maintainer, [Simran Dhindsa](https://github.com/simranxdhindsa), through GitHub. Every report is read and kept private.
